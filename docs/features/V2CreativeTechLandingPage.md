# Feature: V2 Creative Technology Landing Page Route (`/v2`)

## Goal & Overview
The V2 Landing Page (`/v2`) delivers a high-impact, authentic digital experience for J StaR Studios at the intersection of **cinematic filmmaking, full-stack software development, and AI tools**. It discards generic SaaS templates and corporate agency jargon in favor of an opinionated, technical, and art-directed creative technology identity.

## 21st.dev & Interactive Motion Components
- **`NeuralNoiseCanvas`**: Mouse-tracked WebGL organic fluid shader rendered at 60 FPS in electric chartreuse (`#B5E619`).
- **`KineticBorderCard`**: Hardware-accelerated rotating conic gradient border tracing cards at 60 FPS.
- **`PerspectiveTiltCard`**: 3D spring-damped perspective hover with `translateZ` optical parallax.
- **`RadialSpotlightCard`**: Cursor-tracking radial spotlight flare illuminating glass cards.
- **`ColorGradeSlider`**: Interactive DaVinci Resolve before/after slider comparing raw S-Log3 with graded ACES LUT.
- **`TerminalLiveWindow`**: macOS-style studio terminal window with live command execution simulation.
- **`MorphingHeadline`**: Layout-preserving kinetic keyword switcher.
- **`ScrambleText`**: Cybernetic random glyph decoding effect on hover/mount.
- **`V2MagneticButton`**: Magnetic spring physics button tethered to cursor centroid.
