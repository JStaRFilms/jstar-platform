# Physical-device sign-off

Open `https://johnsax.tailb6e2d3.ts.net:3461/redesign` with Tailscale connected. This is the current thirteen-project integration, not the isolated 3460 footer preview.

Record phone model, OS, browser and whether this was a fresh load. Send the project name and a short recording for any failure. Do not submit a real enquiry during this review.

## Phone review

- Cold-load the page. Check headline/font appearance, film startup when it enters and the eager tablet image. Note blank time, stalls or heat.
- Scroll forward and backward with browser toolbars both shown and collapsed. Check for white strips, clipping or unexpected jumps around film, tablet, gallery, team and footer.
- Work should choose the first project. Studio should go directly to the team intro. Start a Project should reach usable form fields.
- Reach the last project, Meet Monjolaoluwa!, using the gallery. Try Film and All filters. Open, close and reopen a film without losing selection or scroll position.
- In the fixed sample only, choose an answer, switch preview/sample modes, enter/exit fullscreen and return later. Check the answer and sample scroll position. Do not use or test the full Study Game app; Try app stays unavailable.
- Play a native film and a YouTube film. Check pause, seek, mute, fullscreen and return. Open notes while playing, then close notes; playback should stay paused. Controls must stay outside the YouTube iframe.
- If an ad appears, record that case rather than assuming the no-ad QA sample covers it. Check the controls and return path through the ad and content transition.
- On an available portrait film, check rotation/fullscreen behaviour supported by your browser. Safari needs its own actual-device result.
- Read Olamide and the final group before enquiry. Check footer opening during entry, stacked mobile lettering and the right-side mobile logo.
- Type a disposable draft without submitting. Open/close the keyboard, rotate or resize where possible, visit the footer and return. Check draft/focus retention and that fields are not covered.
- Turn on reduced motion and check the equivalent controls and destinations.

## Screen reader

Use VoiceOver, TalkBack or a desktop screen reader if available. Check navigation names, selected-project announcements, opening/closing viewer and detail dialogs, focus return, player controls and labelled form fields. Report duplicate hidden content or focus escaping a dialog. The machine accessibility-tree check does not replace this test.

## Not approved by this checklist

A successful phone review does not authorize a real contact submission, full-app testing, public deployment, changing Tailscale routes, deleting archives, staging or committing files. Production build, enquiry delivery and rollout approval remain separate.
