# Portfolio content finalisation

## Objective
Finish the thirteen-project portfolio's factual copy and project-specific credits without reopening the accepted visual direction. Work from the owner's existing accounts, then interview only for missing facts.

## Agent setup
Stage: Build finalisation. Role: orchestrator for the owner interview and factual review; direct bounded edits after confirmation. Follow the existing contribution guide and apply unslop to public copy. No new delegation or deployment is required for intake.

Prime context:
- `src/content/selected-work.ts`, the current live roster and copy.
- `docs/Mockups/01_Public_Website/portfolio-content-ledger.md`, existing owner accounts and evidence.
- `docs/Mockups/01_Public_Website/portfolio-selected-film-notes.md`, the nine additions and missing accounts.
- `docs/features/PortfolioContributionGuide.md`, approval and factual evidence rules.
- `docs/features/PortfolioViewerIntegration.md`, accepted implementation and preservation constraints.

## Scope
Audit existing facts before asking the owner. Complete origin, goal, creative decisions and exact roles for the nine YouTube additions. Confirm Melo's contributor roster and any outstanding software credit wording. Ask for collaborator identities and links only where the owner wants to credit them. Keep unknown details out of public copy.

Gather genuine software screenshots only when the owner supplies them or explicitly authorises an available product for capture. Study Game's broken full app remains outside scope. Its existing screenshot and persistent demonstration stay unchanged.

Use small interview batches, beginning with Books and Bills. Convert rough owner accounts into brief, readable notes, then get copy approval before changing the corresponding public record. Do not silently interpret approval of media cuts or UI as approval of invented project stories.

## Definition of done

Names, roles and links are exact; channel ownership is not treated as production work. Deferred assets and unknowns remain clearly recorded. Relevant checks pass, with no unrelated UI or media changes.

## Constraints
Preserve the roster order, gallery clock, filters, accepted glass/motion, player/quiz DOM and state, original film sources, hero/team/footer and enquiry drafts. No fabricated identities, dates, metrics, commissions, biographies or music permissions. No staged changes, commits, push, deployment, database mutations, full Study Game access or preview-route changes.

Review each account against the ledger before integrating it. Run focused lint/type checks when TypeScript changes. Flag image permissions, missing contributors and publication/export uncertainty separately. Content completion hands off to final device, accessibility and performance QA, not automatic release.
